Day Type Focus

Upper Pull

Day A
Strength focus. Seated cable row, lat pulldown, TRX row, face pull, ER work. 3 to 4 sets of 8 to 12 reps. 60 to 90 second rest.
Day B
Endurance focus. Higher rep, lighter load. Band pull-apart, sidelying ER, Y-T-W, hammer curl. 3 sets of 15 to 20 reps. 30 to 45 second rest.
Day C
Functional and serratus focus. LT wall liftoff, serratus punch, wall clocks, wall slide with band. Motor pattern and scapular control emphasis.

Upper Push

Day A
Scapular plane strength. Cable lateral raise, D2 extension, half-kneeling cable press, tricep work. 3 sets of 8 to 12 reps.
Day B
Endurance and diagonal patterns. Seated DB press, cable low to high lift, band press, glute bridge with serratus punch. 3 sets of 15 reps.
Day C
Sling integration. D2 flexion, cable chop, serratus punch standing, wall clocks progression. Functional movement emphasis.

Lower Strength

Day A
Compound strength. Bilateral leg press, hip thrust with band, RDL, single leg press, hamstring curl, hip ABD machine, step up.
Day B
Posterior chain and hip ER emphasis. Cable pull-through, standing clam, lateral band walk, hip ADD machine, LAQ on leg extension machine.
Day C
Hip stability and single leg focus. Kickstand SL RDL, TRX squat, wall sit with ball squeeze, standing cable hip ER, step up progression.

Lower Endurance

Day A
Tempo and unilateral work. Goblet squat 4-3-1 tempo, luggage squat, split squat with band, kickstand RDL, TRX lateral lunge. 3 sets of 12 to 20 reps.
Day B
Functional and hip flexor focus. Reverse lunge from step, cable pull-through, eccentric hip flexor lowering, prone hamstring curl, wall sit. 3 sets of 15 reps.
Day C
Balance and hip stability. Star excursion, lateral hurdle, TRX SL squat assist, 4 way kicks, standing cable hip ER. Proprioceptive emphasis.

Core and Balance

Day A
Stability and anti-movement. Deadbug, Pallof press, bear crawl hold, plank variations, bird dog, foam roller thoracic extension.
Day B
Trunk slings. Glute bridge with serratus punch, cable chop and lift, Pallof with rotation, eccentric hip flexion with rotation, thread the needle.
Day C
Balance and proprioception. SL foam balance with ball circles, BOSU black side up, star excursion, reactive ball toss, tandem walk, open book.
Guiding Principles

Schedule a check-in with Kelly every other week to review progress, adjust loads, and progress exercises. Do not self-progress. If a session feels off, reduce load and focus on form. Your body will tell you when something is wrong. Learn to listen to it.

Your Goals

The foundation of your program

Primary Goals
01

Longevity and Independence

Train to stay strong, mobile, and fully independent for decades to come. Every session is an investment in your future self.

02

Rebuild and Surpass

Your joint replacements gave you your function back. This program is going to take that foundation and build you stronger and more capable than you have been in years.

03

Stay Active on Your Terms

Keep doing the things you love. Consistent progress that keeps life fully open.

04

Master Your Mechanics

Understand how your body moves and compensates. The goal is not just strength but movement quality that protects your joints long-term.

05

Move With Confidence

Get up and down from the floor with ease. Navigate stairs without thinking about it. Move through your day with the confidence that your body will hold you.

Training Philosophy

This program is built around your specific anatomy and surgical history. All of it informs how we train. None of it holds you back.

We prioritize posterior chain strength, scapular control, hip stability, and proprioception. We build stability before mobility and motor patterns before load. Every exercise is here for a reason.

Precautions

Know before you train

Stop Immediately If

Red Flags — Stop and Contact Kelly

  • Sharp or sudden pain in either shoulder during pressing or lifting
  • New knee pain or swelling that was not present before the session
  • Loss of balance or near-fall during any exercise
  • Pain greater than 4/10 that does not settle within 10 minutes of stopping
  • Any sensation of shoulder instability or clunking
Shoulder Precautions

Right Shoulder (R rTSA — 4 months post-op)

  • No heavy anterior load. No chest press, dumbbell bench, or fly variations
  • No wide grip on any exercise
  • All pressing in the scapular plane (30 to 45 degrees forward of frontal plane)
  • Overhead load increases only under Kelly's supervision
  • Watch for upper trap hiking. Cue shoulder blade down and back
Knee Precautions

Bilateral Knee OA with Valgus

  • Monitor for knee cave (knees falling inward) on all squat and lunge patterns
  • If knee pain increases during loading, reduce load or range of motion
  • Inform Kelly if knee swelling increases after sessions
Ankle and Foot

Fused Left Ankle

  • No standing calf raise on left leg
  • Rear foot support required for split squat. Use band at rack, not foot on bench
  • Heel elevation under left foot for goblet squat and squat patterns
  • Always wear custom orthotics and supportive shoes for all training
Compensation Patterns to Avoid

Watch For These

  • Upper trap hiking during shoulder elevation. Let the shoulder blade move first
  • Toe gripping during balance work. Spread and relax the toes
  • Lumbar extension instead of glute activation during hip thrust. Rib cage down
  • Trunk rotation during bent over rows. The back stays still, only the arm moves
  • Knee valgus collapse during single leg work. Push the knee out over the second toe
  • Spinal flexion instead of hip hinge during RDL. Hinge at the hip, back stays long
Pain Guidelines

0 to 3/10: Acceptable. Continue with attention to form.
4 to 5/10: Modify. Reduce load or range of motion.
6+/10: Stop. Rest and contact Kelly.
